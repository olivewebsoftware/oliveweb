Notice of service discontinuation

We regret to inform you that as of September 3rd, 2025, OliveWeb CONNECT online services are no longer available. Due to security concerns and slow active development, running this service any longer would not be possible.

We apologize for any inconvenience this transition may cause. Thank you to our CONNECT community for utilizing the service.

Affected products:
  • OliveWeb Messenger
  • OliveWeb Accounts
  • Olive OS SE Online Features
  • OliveWeb Chat Rooms

If you would like a copy of your chat history on Messenger, please contact OliveWeb support. Your user data will be securely erased from our servers.
